WaymarX Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

Freedom Automation, Inc. ("Freedom Automation", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the WaymarX mobile application.

Important: WaymarX stores user-created application data locally on your device. Freedom Automation does not operate a cloud service or server that receives or stores your WaymarX pins, locations, photos, groups, notes, or other user-created WaymarX content.

Information Used by WaymarX

WaymarX may use information and device features necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • Location information: WaymarX may use your device's location services to determine your location when creating pins, displaying locations, or providing location-related features.
  • Photos: You may choose to attach photos to pins or other app content.
  • User-created content: Information such as pin names, notes, groups, dates, coordinates, favorites, and related app data may be created and stored within WaymarX.
  • Imported and exported data: WaymarX may allow you to import or export application data using files that you select or create.

How Your WaymarX Data Is Stored

User-created WaymarX data is stored locally on your device using the app's local storage system.

Freedom Automation does not automatically upload your pins, locations, photos, notes, groups, or other WaymarX content to Freedom Automation servers.

If you choose to export or share data from WaymarX, that information may leave your device through the destination or service you select. Any data handled by another service is subject to that service's privacy practices.

Location Data

WaymarX may request permission to use your device's location services. Location information is used to provide location-based app functionality, such as creating and displaying pins.

Location information used by WaymarX for user-created content is stored locally on your device and is not automatically transmitted to Freedom Automation.

You can manage location permissions through your device's privacy settings.

Photos

If you choose to add a photo within WaymarX, the photo may be stored locally on your device as part of your WaymarX content.

WaymarX does not automatically upload those photos to Freedom Automation.

Advertising and Google AdMob

WaymarX may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information in connection with advertising, such as device information, advertising identifiers where permitted, IP address, app interaction data, diagnostic information, and other information used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related advertising purposes.

The information collected or processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's own privacy policies and advertising practices.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, WaymarX may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy notices or consent choices related to advertising.

Available privacy choices may vary depending on your region and applicable legal requirements.

Tracking and Advertising Identifiers

Depending on device settings, operating system permissions, regional requirements, and the configuration of advertising services, Google AdMob or its advertising partners may use advertising-related identifiers or similar technologies.

Apple device privacy controls may allow you to limit or manage tracking and advertising permissions.

Third-Party Services

WaymarX may interact with third-party services when you choose to export, import, share, navigate, or use other system-provided features.

Freedom Automation is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party applications, websites, or services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because WaymarX user-created data is stored locally on your device, you generally control that data through the app and your device.

Deleting content within WaymarX may remove that content from the app's local storage. Deleting the application may also remove locally stored app data, subject to normal device backup and operating-system behavior.

Data that you have independently exported, shared, backed up, or transferred to another service must be managed through that destination or service.

Children's Privacy

WaymarX is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Freedom Automation does not operate a WaymarX user-account or cloud database that knowingly collects children's user-created WaymarX content.

Security

WaymarX relies on Apple's operating system and device security mechanisms for protection of locally stored application data.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

California and Other Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide certain privacy rights regarding personal information.

Because Freedom Automation does not operate a WaymarX server that stores user-created WaymarX content, Freedom Automation generally does not possess the locally stored pins, photos, notes, groups, or location records created by users within the app.

Information processed independently by Google or other third-party services is subject to those providers' privacy processes and policies.

International Users

Privacy and advertising requirements may vary by country or region. WaymarX may use Google's privacy and consent tools to provide notices and choices where required.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to WaymarX, applicable laws, or third-party services used by the application.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
