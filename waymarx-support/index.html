Getting started

WaymarX helps you remember locations you discover and return to them later. Here are the essentials.

Use the app's location or pin-drop tools to mark a spot and save its details for later.

🗂️ Organize your spots

🧭 Return to a location

Open a saved place to view its location and use the available map or navigation options.

Frequently asked questions

Why isn't my current location updating?

Make sure Location Services are enabled for your iPhone and that WaymarX has location permission. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, then reopen the app and try refreshing the location. Location accuracy can vary indoors or where GPS reception is limited.

Why didn't I receive a location reminder?

Geofence reminders depend on iOS location permissions, notification settings, device conditions, and location accuracy. Check that notifications are allowed for WaymarX and that the app has the location access it requests. Arrival alerts may not occur immediately or in every circumstance.

Can I mark a place as visited without a reminder?

Open the saved location in WaymarX and look for its available status or organization actions. You don't need to rely on a geofence notification to manage your saved places.

Why is the pin in a slightly different place?

Phone location measurements have a margin of error, especially indoors, near tall structures, or with weak satellite visibility. Check the location shown before saving an important spot.

How do I report a problem or request a feature?

Email our support team with a short description, your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the WaymarX app version. Screenshots can help. Please don't include passwords or sensitive personal information.
